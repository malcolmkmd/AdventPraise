piano.sf2 is the acoustic grand piano (General MIDI program 0, "Yamaha Grand
Piano") from the Fluid (R3) General MIDI SoundFont, FluidR3_GM.sf2, as shipped
in Debian's fluid-soundfont-gm 3.1-6 package. Each stereo sample pair was mixed
to mono and resampled to 24 kHz (tools/extract_piano.py); nothing else changed.
