Advent Praise puts Christ In Song in your pocket in English, Chichewa, Tswana, Xhosa, Sotho, Shona, Venda, Zulu and Ndebele, together with the SDA Hymnal (1985). Every hymnal is free, there are no ads, and there is no account to sign up for.

FIND A HYMN IN SECONDS
Type its number on the large number pad and the title appears before you tap Go. Or search by the words you remember: search covers numbers, titles and lyrics, and ignores accents, capitals and apostrophes.

THE SDA HYMNAL (1985)
All 695 hymns of the SDA Hymnal sit beside Christ In Song as their own option, with their own numbering. Switch between them and Advent Praise keeps you on the same hymn wherever the two books share it.

READ IT YOUR WAY
• Five reading themes: Paper, Parchment, Sepia, Muted and Black
• Serif or sans-serif text, with adjustable size, spacing and alignment
• Large print for easier reading
• The chorus after every verse, if you like
• Swipe for the next or previous hymn, and tap to hide everything but the words

CHURCH MODE
Show one verse at a time in large, bright text, sized to fill the screen, so the whole congregation can follow. The chorus comes back after every verse, and at the last verse you can go straight to the next hymn. The screen stays awake for the whole service.

PLAN THE SERVICE
• Favourites for each hymnal
• Playlists, kept between launches, so you can prepare the order of service ahead of time
• Service plans and themed collections
• Recent hymns, one tap away
• Hymn of the Day and Church Remote widgets for your Home screen

ADVENT PRAISE PRO
Pro adds the tools for leading worship:
• Unlimited piano accompaniment to sing along with, offline
• Sheet music to read and play from, in page or line view, in step with the piano
• A clean worship display on a TV or projector, through Google Cast or a connected screen
• Church mode controls in your notifications
• The pitch pipe

You can try the piano a few times for free first. Pro is an annual subscription with a 3-day free trial for new subscribers. Payment is taken through your Google Play account when the trial ends, and the subscription renews each year until you cancel. Cancel any time in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions, before the trial or the current year ends, and you won't be charged again.
